FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the AI background removal?
Our AI achieves high accuracy for most common subjects including people, products, animals, and objects. For complex images (intricate hair, transparent objects, similar foreground/background colors), you can use our manual refinement tools to perfect the result. Quality depends on the original image quality and the AI model in use.
What file formats are supported?
AntryGravity supports JPG, JPEG, PNG, and WEBP input formats. You can export results as transparent PNG, JPG, or WEBP. File size limits and resolution caps depend on your subscription plan.
How does the credit system work?
Each AI operation costs a certain number of credits (configurable by admin). Background removal costs 1 credit by default. Credits are deducted only after a successful processing job. Failed jobs are eligible for refunds based on the configured refund policy. Credits do not roll over to the next month on the Free plan.
Is there an API for developers?
Yes! Business and Enterprise plans include API access. You can create API keys in your dashboard, integrate background removal into your own applications, and track usage through our API dashboard. See the API documentation for details.
Are my images stored permanently?
Processed images are stored in your project workspace and accessible as long as your account is active. Temporary processing files are automatically cleaned up after 24 hours. You can delete any project and its associated images at any time from your dashboard.
Can I use AntryGravity for commercial projects?
Yes! All paid plans (Pro, Business, Enterprise) grant you full commercial usage rights for images processed through AntryGravity. The Free plan also permits commercial use, subject to the watermark policy. Please review our Terms of Service for complete details.
