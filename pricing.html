FAQ

Pricing Questions

Can I change my plan anytime?
Yes! You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.
Do credits roll over?
Credits reset monthly on all plans. Unused credits do not roll over. You can purchase additional credits at any time if you run out.
Is there a free trial for Pro?
The Free plan lets you try all core features. Pro features can be evaluated during a 7-day trial when you first upgrade — contact support to activate a trial.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit/debit cards and PayPal through our secure payment processor. We never store your card details directly.
Are refunds available?
Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time. Refunds for subscription charges are available within 14 days of purchase if you haven't used your credits. See our Refund Policy for full details.
How does Enterprise pricing work?
Enterprise pricing is customized based on your volume, team size, and feature requirements. Contact our sales team for a personalized quote and demo.
